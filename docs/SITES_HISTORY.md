## Dependencies and rounding

F22 = (F17+F18+F19+F20)*100. F25 = ROUND(F23*F24/F22,1).
G17/G18 = ROUND(percent*F25*100,1). G19/G20 = ROUND(percent*F25*100,0).
Leaven build base = G17*J17 (bread flour, not all flour). K19:K22 = ROUND(base * each J19:J22 percentage,0). K17 is their sum.
H29 = ROUND(G17-K20-K19/2,0). H30 = ROUND(G18-K21,0). H31 = ROUND(G19-K22-K19/2,0). H32 = G20. H33 = K17. H35 is their sum.
The culture subtraction assumes half bread flour and half water, irrespective of the leaven build hydration. Preserve this assumption visibly.
Autolyse: F2 = ROUND(H29*.88), F3=H30, F4=ROUND(H31*.90). Final addition: F5=ROUND(H29*.12), F6=H33, F7=H32, F8=ROUND(H31*.10). These splits are formula constants, not separate input cells. Preserve as constants.
F10 is the sum of F2:F8, F11=F19, F13=F4+F8, F14=F2+F3+F5.
Manual recipe total is sum of its gram inputs; hydration = total added water / total added flour, excluding leaven. Blends only sum flour in source. They have no dependency into other recipes.
All percentage inputs remain independent. Warn on unusual totals or negative resulting quantities; do not normalize or silently alter formulas.
Google Sheets ROUND is half away from zero. Preserve this for negative intermediate cases as well.
