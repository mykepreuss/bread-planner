# How Bread Planner behaves

This is the contract for the rebuild: what the app did at commit `34699a7`, written from the code and checked against the recorded outputs, and kept since except where Michael decided otherwise. His decisions are at the end; sections that changed say so.

Two records back it up. `npm test` checks the code against `tests/baseline/baseline.json` (164 plans, 42 recipe calculations, 24 kinds of saved data), including `App.tsx` rendered for each plan. `npm run test:browser` checks the built site against 22 recorded screens, each at phone and desktop width, and 35 recorded actions.

## Ingredients

Each recipe stores: loaf count and weight, added hydration, leaven used and salt (each a percentage of added flour), the share of water held back for the final mix, the flour parts, and a leaven build in grams per 1,000 g of added flour (mature starter, flour, whole wheat, water).

- **Weights.** Total dough is loaves × weight. Added flour is total ÷ (1 + (hydration + leaven + salt) ÷ 100). Water, leaven used and salt follow from their percentages. Each flour gets its share of the parts. A flour with 0 parts is listed as "Optional · not used" and left out of the mixing lists.
- **Mixing stages.** House method: the autolyse gets the early flours and the water not held back, and the final mix gets rye, leaven used, salt and the held-back water. Tartine: the initial mix gets leaven used, the flours and the first water, and the second addition gets salt and the remaining water. At the defaults these are exactly House 800/100/680 g then 100/200/20/100 g, and Tartine 200/900/100/700 g then 20/50 g. Tartine Whole Wheat has an autolyse instead (decision 19), so its stages are House's: "01 / Autolyse" with 700/300/750 g, then "02 / Final mix" with 200 g leaven, 20 g salt and 50 g water.
- **Leaven.** The build is scaled by added flour ÷ 1,000. The app shows Made, Used in dough, and Reserved or Short by. A deficit under a millionth of a gram counts as none. A real deficit shows "Make N g more leaven before mixing."
- **Total hydration** counts the flour and water in the leaven used. Mature starter is taken as half flour and half water; an empty build is taken as 100% hydration. The field shows a whole number, and typing a value sets the added hydration that gives it, so the dough weight stays the same. Its lowest accepted value is the total hydration with no added water.
- **Controls.** Hydration, leaven, salt and water held back have both a slider and a number field. The sliders run 50–100%, 0–50%, 0–5% and 0–100%, and widen to show a value typed outside that range. An invalid entry shows its allowed range and reverts when you leave the field. Loaf count must be a whole number of 1 or more.
- **Basket flour** isn't weighed. The Tartine recipes showed a "Basket and dusting flour" card with rice flour at 20% of added flour until November 22, 2026 (decision 12). The bench-rest note and the method guide still say to dust the baskets with rice flour, about 200 g for the book's two loaves.
- **Add-ins.** Olive Bread and Sesame Bread list their add-ins as a third stage, "03 / Add-ins", in the book's measures rather than grams, scaled by the dough's weight against two default loaves (the book's batch is for two) and written to the nearest quarter (3 loaves of olive bread: 4½ cups of olives). Olive: 3 cups pitted olives, 2 cups toasted walnuts or hazelnuts (optional), 2 teaspoons dried herbes de Provence and the grated zest of 1 lemon. Sesame: 1 cup unhulled sesame seeds, toasted and cooled.
- "Restore recipe defaults" resets the selected recipe's settings and checklist, final-mix ticks included, after a confirmation. Other recipes, the dates and the oven settings are kept.

## Schedule

Times below are for a 9:00 AM leaven start with two loaves baked together. Durations are fixed; there is no editor.

**House method:** feed starter at 10:00 PM the evening before, start leaven 9:00, start autolyse 12:30 PM (90 minutes, until the leaven is ready; 9:30 AM for 4 h 30 min until decision 15), final mix 2:00 PM, start bulk 2:15, seven turns every 30 minutes from 2:45, preshape 6:15 (10 min), bench rest 6:25, final shape 6:55, basket rest 7:00, cold proof 7:20 PM for 12 h 30 min. Next morning: take the loaves out and preheat to 540°F at 7:50, bake covered at 460°F 8:50, remove lid 9:10, out 9:33, the last step. Changed at Michael's request (decision 11): a 15-minute cooling mark followed each batch until November 22, 2026; the out-of-the-oven note still says to cool each loaf at least 15 minutes before slicing.

**Tartine** (Tartine Country Bread): make leaven 9:00 AM, mix and rest 9:00 PM, salt, final water and start of warm bulk 9:30, five turns from 10:00, divide and preshape 12:30 AM (5 min), bench rest 12:35, final shape 1:05, then either refrigerate 1:10 for 18 hours (decision 21; the step and the Final proof menu say "about 18 hours", where the book gives 10 to 12) and warm 1 h, or a warm proof of 3 h 30 min. Preheat to 500°F 30 minutes before the bake, and bake at 450°F, 20 minutes covered and 20 uncovered. With a cold proof the loaves are out at 8:50 PM the next day. Its night-free leaven starts are 9:00 to 10:30 PM with a cold proof and 7:00 to 10:30 PM with a warm one: from a 9:30 PM leaven, the initial mix is at 9:30 AM, the salt at 10:00 AM, the fridge at 1:40 PM, and the loaves come out at 7:40 AM and are out of the oven at 9:20 AM two mornings later.

**Olive Bread and Sesame Bread** (decision 16) are Preuss Country Bread's plan, step for step, with one more step at Turn 1's time (2:45 PM), right after it, to work the add-ins in, with the scaled amounts in its note. It counts as part of bulk, like a turn, and its Up next card offers the dough temperature as a turn's does. Sesame also has "Toast the sesame seeds" an hour before Turn 1, at 1:45 PM as the autolyse finishes, lasting until the final mix: about 15 minutes to heat the oven to 400°F, 10 minutes, a stir, 10 to 15 more, and 20 minutes to cool. It is coloured as an oven step, is hands-on (so it shows in quiet hours when it starts after 10:30 PM), and moves nothing.

**Tartine variations.** Country Rye rests 50 minutes after the initial mix by default instead of 30, the middle of the book's 40 to 60, with water at 75°F instead of 80°F, so every later step is 20 minutes later than Tartine Country Bread's. Its cold final proof is 17½ hours rather than 12 (decision 20; the step and the Final proof menu say "about 17½ hours"), so with a cold proof everything after the fridge is 10 minutes sooner than Tartine Country Bread's, whose fridge is 18 hours, and a leaven from 9:00 to 10:30 PM keeps every step out of quiet hours: from a 9:30 PM leaven, the initial mix is at 9:30 AM, the salt at 10:20 AM, the fridge at 2:00 PM, and the loaves come out at 7:30 AM and are out of the oven at 9:10 AM two mornings later. Its warm proof is unchanged.

**Tartine Whole Wheat** (decision 19): make leaven 9:00 AM; "Start autolyse" 7:00 PM for 2 hours, the flours and the first water at 75°F, without the leaven; "Add leaven, salt and final water" and the start of warm bulk 9:00 PM, as the leaven is ready, with Leaven, Salt and Water to tick; five turns from 9:30, divide and preshape 12:00 AM, bench rest 12:05, final shape 12:35, then either refrigerate 12:40 AM for 18½ hours (the step says "about 18½ hours", and so does the Final proof menu) and warm 1 h, or a warm proof of 3 h 30 min. So everything from the leaven going in is 30 minutes sooner than Tartine Country Bread's, and with a cold proof, after a fridge 30 minutes longer than its 18 hours, the loaves come out at the same time, 8:50 PM. Its night-free leaven starts, with either proof, are 9:00 to 10:30 PM: a 9:30 PM leaven has the autolyse at 7:30 AM, the fridge at 1:10 PM and the loaves out at 9:20 AM two mornings later.

- **Turn wording.** House-method turns 1–3 say to go around all four sides twice, vigorously; turns 4–7 are one round. Tartine keeps the book's wording.
- **Step notes** name the selected recipe's ingredients and grams: the leaven build and what is kept, and the autolyse. The final mix lists its ingredients as tick-offs instead (see Bake day), so its note is "Add these after the autolyse. Mix until incorporated.", or "Add these to each bowl…" when mixing in several bowls. Every step has a note. "Keep later batches chilled until their own warm-up" appears only with more than one batch.
- **Mixing bowls.** "Loaves per mix" on the Schedule tab's batch card, 2 by default, is the most loaves of dough one bowl holds; it is shared by every recipe. More loaves than that are mixed in as many equal bowls as it takes (4 loaves at 2 per mix: 2 bowls; 6: 3; 3: 2 bowls of 1.5 loaves), and the card says how many. With more than one bowl, the leaven note says how much goes into each bowl, the autolyse (House) lists one bowl's weights "In each of N bowls", Tartine's initial mix says to split it equally between the bowls, the final mix lists each bowl's ingredients as its own set of tick-offs (Bowl 1, Bowl 2…), and the start of bulk says to combine the bowls in one container. The Ingredients tab's two mixing stages then show one bowl's weights. Times don't change. Added at Michael's request (decision 14).
- **Autolyse length.** Each recipe has its own autolyse length on the Schedule tab's batch card, in whole minutes from 0 to 720, and Restore recipe defaults puts it back. The defaults are the times above: 90 minutes for Preuss Country Bread, Olive Bread and Sesame Bread, 120 for Tartine Whole Wheat. An autolyse always ends as the leaven is ready, so a longer or shorter one moves only its own start; nothing else on the plan moves, and the card says "It ends as the leaven is ready, so only its start moves." Tartine Country Bread and Tartine Country Rye have no autolyse, so the field is "Rest after mixing" instead, the rest with the leaven in before the salt, 30 and 50 minutes by default; every step after it moves with it, and the card says so. Their initial-mix notes give the book's range ("Cover and rest; the book gives 25 to 40 minutes.", or 40 to 60 for Country Rye) instead of fixing a length. Added at Michael's request (decision 22).
- **Batches.** Loaves baked at once is 1 or 2. With more loaves than that, later batches each get a reheat (30 minutes by default, editable once there is more than one batch) and, unless Tartine's proof is warm, their own warm-up. Titles then name the batch and its loaves. One loaf, or two baked together, drop batch numbers ("Bake covered (both loaves)", "Remove lid").
- **Plan around** the leaven start or "All loaves out of the oven" (the last batch). Switching converts the date and time so every step stays where it was.
- **Cold proof.** For House-method recipes, the oven card shows the cold proof's start, end and length, marked as an overnight cold ferment or a daytime proof. A daytime one adds a notice above the steps and renames the step "Cold proof (daytime)". Every House-method recipe calls it "Overnight cold proof" otherwise (decision 2, made when Red Spring & Rye was still on the menu); Tartine calls its own "Refrigerated final proof".
- **Quiet hours** run from after 10:30 PM until 7:00 AM. Hands-on steps that start then are listed in a warning with a badge on each step. Mixing and shaping that run past the cutoff are listed too. Passive steps are not: the start of bulk. Checked and skipped steps drop off the list.
- **A start with less night work.** Before anything is checked, the warning suggests the leaven start with the fewest quiet-hour actions. It tries every quarter hour for 24 hours from 12 hours before the planned start, or from now if that is later, leaving out any whose first step would already be past (House's starter feed comes 11 hours before its leaven), and of those with the fewest keeps the one whose actions fall least far into quiet hours, then the nearest to the plan, the later of two as near. It shows only when that has fewer actions than the plan, and never the plan's own start. "Start at 10:30 PM" moves the leaven there; a plan made from the finish stays so. A Tartine recipe is also offered the other proof's quietest start when it has fewer still. Since every Tartine recipe's cold proof has night-free starts for up to twelve loaves (decisions 19 to 21), that is a cold proof offered to a warm-proofed plan with none, such as eight loaves baked one at a time: "Use a cold proof at 9:00 PM" sets the cold proof and the start. With nothing better, the warning says no other start time has less; once a step is checked it says, as before, to try an earlier start or a different finish time. Added at Michael's request (decision 18).
- **Timeline.** Steps are grouped by day ("Day 1 Saturday, Nov 21"), and within a day they keep the plan's order, which is time order until bake day. Check-offs and the dough temperature move steps but never reorder them within a day, so a step can show a later time than the one below it (a turn checked at 7:15 PM above a preshape still planned for 6:15). A step whose time moves onto another day shows under that day: a turn left unchecked at 11:46 PM stays under day 1, while turns checked after midnight move to day 2. Between steps, a label gives the time to the next step. The cold proof is coloured Fridge blue. The preheat, each reheat, the bakes and each batch out of the oven are Crust brown. Everything else is neutral, including later batches' warm-ups. The header gives the total time and "N of M steps checked".
- **No schedule** for an impossible date or time, a time skipped by daylight saving, a loaf count that is not a whole number of 1 or more, more than 1,000 batches, or a blend with no flour. Elapsed times stay correct across daylight-saving changes. Changed with Michael's decisions: a year before 1000 used to give no schedule too; it now gets one.
- **Method guide.** Below the tabs, three closed sections give fixed guidance: mature starter and readiness, establishing a starter from scratch, and shaping, baskets and baking. Preuss Country Bread, Olive Bread and Sesame Bread, the House-method recipes, add a paragraph on the House method's autolyse and vigorous early turns; the Tartine-method recipes don't.

## Bake day

- **Checking a step** records the time, rounded to the minute. Its "Done at" date and time fields can be corrected; an incomplete entry is ignored. Unchecking removes the time.
- **The plan follows the dough.** A checked step that moves the plan sets how far behind or ahead the plan runs, and every unchecked step after it moves by that amount. The latest such step decides. The starter feed, the autolyse and the turns record their time but move nothing: turns stay on their 30-minute marks from the start of bulk. The Up next card says "Running N behind plan" or "ahead of plan".
- **Skipped.** Any step left unchecked before a later checked step is marked Skipped, except a step the plan starts at the same time as the latest checked one, such as taking the loaves out of the fridge as the oven preheats. A skipped step no longer counts as Up next or quiet-hour work, and can still be checked. Changed at Michael's request (decision 7): only the start of bulk and the turns could be skipped before.
- **A big skip asks first.** A check that would newly mark more than three earlier steps Skipped first asks "Skip N earlier steps?", with "Check and skip" and "Don't check it", and is recorded at the tap. Once the start of bulk is due, the start of bulk and the turns don't count, since skipping them then means the dough was ready early, so "Ready: preshape now" never asks. Before it, they count, so a tap on preshape the evening before asks. Added at Michael's request (decision 8): one mis-tap on the last step passed over the whole bake.
- **Up next** is the first step not checked or skipped. It shows a countdown, refreshed every 30 seconds and whenever the page comes back into view, plus the note, the readiness cue, a Done button and the step after it. It is hidden once the plan has ended.
- **Ended.** A plan has ended when its last batch is out in the past and either nothing is checked or every step is checked or skipped. It then says "This plan ended …" with "Plan the next bake", which moves the date to tomorrow, keeps every setting, and starts a fresh checklist (decision 1).
- **Dough temperature.** Up next shows "Dough temperature after mixing" while the next step is the start of bulk, a turn or preshape. Once a value is in, turns and preshape show "Dough temperature now" with an Update button instead. Each reading is listed under "Start bulk fermentation" with a remove button. A reading in the same minute replaces the earlier one. Readings can be in °F or °C: 50–100°F or 10–37.7°C, to any decimal, stored in °F (decision 5; the °C field used to accept up to 38°C, and a later reading only in half-degree steps). A saved temperature shown in °C is rounded to a tenth, so 100°F shows as 37.8°C, without an error.
- **Bulk estimate.** The planned bulk counts as dough at 78°F, and time doubles for every 15°F cooler and halves for every 15°F warmer. The reading after mixing applies from the start of bulk, and each later reading from when it was taken, so a new reading re-times only what is left. Readings from before this bake's final mix are ignored; one between the final mix and the start of bulk counts from the start.
- **The estimate moves the plan.** Preshape moves to the estimated end of bulk, never before the latest checked turn, and every later step keeps its spacing. Turns stay on 30-minute marks. A turn due less than 15 minutes before preshape is dropped. Cool dough gains at most two extra turns, and checked turns always stay. Once preshape or a later step is checked, the temperature moves nothing. The timeline then shows the turns that were due before preshape was done, plus any checked ones, and the note says how long bulk took against the estimate.
- **Advice.** The note gives the estimated length and ready time, and says to start checking 30 minutes earlier. Within 15 minutes of the plan it says so and stops there. Further off, it says which way the plan moved and how many turns it now has, adds "keep folding every 30 minutes" for cool dough (capped at one more hour), and names the steady temperature that would preshape at the original time, if that is between 68°F and 86°F.
- **Dough check.** From 30 minutes before the estimated end of bulk, or before preshape if no temperature was entered, a turn's Up next card leads with "Check the dough before this turn" and "Ready: preshape now", which checks preshape.
- **Final-mix tick-offs.** The step where the last ingredients go in lists each one with its weight and its own check box, in the Up next card and on the timeline: rye flour, leaven, salt and water for Preuss Country Bread's "Mix final dough", and salt and water for a Tartine recipe's "Add salt and final water". An ingredient the recipe has none of is left out, and with none at all the House note is just "Mix until incorporated." A tick is saved with the bake. It records no time, moves nothing, and neither checks the step nor is needed to check it; checking the step leaves the ticks as they are, so one still not ticked shows on the step afterwards and can be ticked there. Added at Michael's request (decision 13).
- **Clearing.** "Clear checklist" and "Plan the next bake" clear the selected recipe's checks, times, final-mix ticks and dough temperatures. A new date or time while nothing is checked clears the dough temperatures and final-mix ticks. Clear checklist is available once a step is checked or an ingredient is ticked.
- **Phone layout.** At 850 px and below, once a step is checked and the plan has not ended, the step list moves above the batch and oven cards.
- When every step is checked or skipped, the timeline ends with "All batches done. Enjoy your bread."

## Saved data

- Everything is saved in the browser under `bread-planner.v6` on every change, as `{version: 6, plan}`. Each recipe keeps its own settings, checklist, check-off times, final-mix ticks, dough temperature and readings. The selected recipe, date, time, plan-around choice, Tartine proof, loaves at once, loaves per mix, reheat time and temperature unit are shared.
- Changed in step 3b of the rebuild: until then the planner saved version 5 under `bread-planner.v5`. When there is no usable version 6 save, the planner upgrades the version 5 one. It never writes or removes `bread-planner.v5`, so a rollback to an earlier release finds the plan as it was when it was upgraded. Edits made on that earlier release go only to version 5, so moving forward again opens the version 6 save without them (decision 6).
- Not saved: the open tab and the open sections. A reload opens the Schedule tab with every section closed.
- When browser storage can't be read, the planner starts fresh. When it can't be written, the planner keeps working without saving.
- On loading either version, a value that is missing or invalid falls back to its default:
  - Settings must be finite numbers of 0 or more. Loaf count and weight must be at least 1, the loaf count whole, the water held back at most 100%, and the autolyse whole minutes up to 720. Unknown settings are dropped.
  - A recipe saved without an autolyse length, as every save before November 23, 2026 is, gets the recipe's default. The save is still version 6: an earlier release ignores the length and leaves it out when it next saves.
  - Check-off times are kept only for checked steps. Readings outside 50–100°F are dropped, and readings sort by time.
  - Final-mix ticks are a list of ids such as `mix:salt`, each kept once. A save without the list, as every save before November 22, 2026 is, loads with nothing ticked, and so does anything that isn't a list. The save is still version 6: an earlier release ignores the list and leaves it out when it next saves.
  - The recipe order keeps known recipes once each, in the saved order, followed by every recipe it doesn't name in menu order, so a recipe added since the save is on the menu (decision 10; before, a recipe the save didn't name was left off, and an empty order became just Preuss Country Bread). The selected recipe must be in the order; otherwise the first recipe is selected.
  - Loaves at once is capped at 2.
  - Loaves per mix must be a whole number of 1 or more; a save without it, as every save before November 22, 2026 is, mixes 2.
  - A missing or invalid date falls back to tomorrow and a time to 9:00 AM, as for a new plan (decision 4). A date must be a real calendar date and a time must be from 00:00 to 23:59. Changed in step 4: before, any date shaped like `YYYY-MM-DD` and time shaped like `HH:MM` was kept, so a damaged save could open a plan with no steps. Until the decisions of November 21, 2026, the fallback was the date and time the plan was first made, which the save then also kept; an older save's creation date is now ignored.
  - A House blend saved with red spring flour carries its parts over to Anita's.
  - Olive Bread or Sesame Bread saved as a Tartine variation, whose version 6 settings name a flour it no longer uses (white-bread flour), loads fresh: its settings at their defaults and an empty checklist, so the two doughs don't mix (decision 16). The rule covers those two recipes only, so a later change to another recipe's flours can't wipe its save.
  - A save of any other version starts fresh. Before step 3b, versions 1 to 4 were upgraded; that upgrade is not carried into the rebuild (see below).

## Not carried into the rebuild

No saved data on this site can reach these, because every version of the app here before step 3b saved version 5:

- the hidden "Red Spring & Rye Variation" recipe, which is listed only once old spreadsheet cells are edited;
- upgrades from saved versions 1 to 4;
- the spreadsheet cells and durations stored inside each save, which never change from their defaults (so a preshape saved as 5 minutes, the default before November 12, no longer needs turning into 10);
- the checklists kept in the old part of a save, used only for a recipe with no entry of its own. Every version 5 save holds all seven recipes, and those lists are always empty.

## Decisions for Michael

The rebuild kept each of these as the app had them until Michael chose. He decided the first six on November 21, 2026, and asked for the seventh to fourteenth on November 22; the rules above follow his choices.

1. **Next bake after a finished one.** On a plan with every step checked, "Plan the next bake" moved the date to tomorrow but kept the checklist, so the plan still showed as ended. **Decided: it now starts a fresh checklist**, so the next bake begins with nothing checked. The browser record's flow changed with it.
2. **Cold proof name.** Red Spring & Rye called its cold proof "Refrigerated final proof" and left "overnight" out of its note; the other House-method recipes say "Overnight cold proof". **Decided: it uses the House wording**, including "Cold proof (daytime)" for a proof within one day. Superseded on November 22, 2026: Red Spring & Rye was removed (decision 9).
3. **Checklists per recipe.** Each recipe keeps its own checklist and temperatures, while the date and time are shared. **Decided: kept as it is.**
4. **A corrupt saved date** fell back to the date the save was first created. **Decided: it falls back to tomorrow, and a time to 9:00 AM, as for a new plan.** The save no longer keeps a creation date.
5. **38°C was accepted, then lost.** The °C field accepted 10–38°C, but saved temperatures must be 50–100°F, and 38°C is 100.4°F, so a 38°C reading disappeared on reload. **Decided: the °C field accepts 10–37.7°C**, and 38°C shows the allowed range.
6. **Moving forward after a rollback.** If the site is rolled back to a release before step 3b and then moved forward again, edits made in between are in version 5 only, and the planner opens its version 6 save without them. The alternative was to keep a fingerprint of the version 5 save in version 6, and upgrade version 5 again whenever it changed. **Decided November 21, 2026: kept as it is.** Michael is the planner's only user, so it matters only if he rolls the site back and edits a plan before moving forward again.
7. **An unchecked early step held Up next back.** Only the start of bulk and the turns counted as skipped, so with Feed starter unchecked and every step through Turn 3 checked, Up next still showed Feed starter. **Decided November 22, 2026: any step left unchecked before a later checked step is skipped**, so Up next shows Turn 4. A forgotten early step also no longer keeps a finished plan from ending. A step the plan starts at the same time as the checked one stays open: Preheat checked first leaves "Take both loaves out of the fridge" as Up next. 32 of the record's 214 plans changed with it.
8. **One mis-tap passed over the whole bake.** Checking the last step on a fresh plan marked the 22 steps before it Skipped, hid Up next and showed "All batches done". **Decided November 22, 2026: a check that would skip more than three earlier steps asks first**, not counting the start of bulk and the turns once bulk is due. The record didn't change; the browser record gained a flow for it.
9. **Six recipes.** The menu listed Preuss House, Original Tartine, White & Whole Wheat, Red Spring & Rye, Five-Flour Sourdough and Four-Flour Sourdough. **Decided November 22, 2026: only Preuss House and Original Tartine remain.** The red spring, all-purpose and Red Fife flours went with them, and so did "Uses the House leaven, salt and overnight method.", which only Five-Flour and Four-Flour showed. The record dropped those recipes' 94 plans and 19 calculations; the other 120 plans, 26 calculations and 53 helper results are unchanged. Each of the 24 saved-data cases lost the four recipes, and the two that had one selected now open on Preuss House.
10. **Four recipes from Tartine Bread.** **Decided November 22, 2026: Tartine Olive (page 88), Tartine Sesame (page 90), Tartine Whole Wheat (page 114) and Tartine Country Rye (page 118) are on the menu.** Their notes and descriptions are written for the app, not copied from the book. Choices made for him, each easy to change: add-ins stay in the book's cups and teaspoons; the toast step starts an hour before Turn 1 to allow for heating the oven; Whole Wheat and Country Rye rest 50 minutes and hold back 50 g of water for the salt. A saved menu now gains recipes it doesn't name, so the new ones show on a device with an older save. Olive and Sesame became Preuss House variations in decision 16. Whole Wheat's rest was replaced by an autolyse in decision 19.
11. **No cooling mark.** Every bake ended with a "15-minute cooling mark" step, one per batch. **Decided November 22, 2026: removed.** Each batch now ends when it comes out of the oven, and the last step is "All loaves out of the oven" ("Loaf out of the oven" for one loaf). A saved check of a cooling mark is no longer counted. The record lost 256 cooling-mark steps across its 154 plans.
